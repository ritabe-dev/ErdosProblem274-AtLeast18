# Literature review request

Please check the literature boundary for the following candidate statement:

> Every nontrivial coset partition of an arbitrary group into at most
> seventeen cells has repeated subgroup index.

Questions:

1. Is an arbitrary-group result for any of the cell counts eight through
   seventeen already published, posted, or known as folklore?
2. Has the Margolis-Schnabel obstruction set been combined with a complete
   Egyptian-fraction enumeration and an index-four fiber obstruction before?
3. Does Akman-Sissokho Theorem 6 give the arbitrary-group result through seven
   distinct subgroups used in the comparison?
4. Do corrections, theses, non-English papers, or private notes alter this
   comparison?
5. Are the two partition identities, four obstruction statements, and their
   proposition locators represented accurately?
